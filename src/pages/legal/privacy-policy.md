This data is used solely to improve website usability and user experience. Microsoft may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy, available at [https://privacy.microsoft.com](https://privacy.microsoft.com). No personally identifiable information is intentionally captured through Clarity.

When you make a payment or book a service through our website, your payment and appointment information is handled by Square in accordance with their privacy policy, available at [https://squareup.com/us/en/legal/general/privacy](https://squareup.com/us/en/legal/general/privacy).
